Executive summary

I built a structured way to approach pricing decisions on customized advertising solutions. The work improved the consistency and efficiency of pricing operations, and quote turnaround went from weeks to minutes.

Business challenge

Customized advertising solutions required a scalable approach to commercial pricing decisions that balanced customer value, competitiveness, and revenue objectives.

My role

  • Evaluated customer-value dimensions and the commercial trade-offs behind pricing decisions
  • Developed a structured framework for pricing discussions
  • Partnered with sales, planning, and product stakeholders to streamline decision-making

Analytical approach

I started by understanding the constraints each stakeholder group worked under, then built the framework around them so it would be useful in day-to-day decisions. I evaluated customer-value dimensions and assessed commercial trade-offs before settling on the structure.

Outcome

  • Helped improve the consistency and efficiency of pricing operations while supporting business growth
  • Quote turnaround fell from weeks to minutes
  • The framework became a standing reference for the team

Lessons learned

  • Start with people’s constraints. A framework gets used when it fits the pressures stakeholders actually face.
  • Consistency and speed can go together. A clear structure means fewer decisions have to start from scratch.
  • Influence without authority. Aligning stakeholders mattered as much as the analysis.
